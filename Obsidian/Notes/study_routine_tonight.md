# Tonight's Study Routine
## How to Actually Remember Everything by Tomorrow

---

## 🎯 TOTAL TIME NEEDED: 90 minutes
(Can be done in 3x 30-min sessions if you want breaks)

---

## 📚 PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (20 minutes)

### Step 1: Read & Understand the Pattern (10 min)
Read the "Pattern Method" section from the Quick Reference:
- The 4 cl rule
- Build method steps
- Glass selection rules
- Citrus patterns

**Active task**: Write these patterns down by hand (seriously - writing = remembering)

### Step 2: Spirit Families (10 min)
Study ONE spirit family at a time:
1. Read vodka drinks
2. Close your eyes and recite them
3. Move to gin
4. Repeat

**Don't memorize individual recipes yet** - just understand the families.

---

## 🧠 PHASE 2: ACTIVE RECALL DRILL (40 minutes)

This is where you ACTUALLY learn. Use the Substitution Guide as your quiz.

### Round 1: Flashcard Style (20 min)
Cover the recipe, read the drink name, try to say:
1. Glass type
2. Base spirit amount
3. What's added
4. What tops it
5. Garnish

**Start with vodka drinks** (7 drinks):
- Screwdriver → "Highball, 4 cl vodka, orange juice, lime"
- Cape Codder → "Highball, 4 cl vodka, cranberry, lime"
- Keep going...

**Then gin drinks** (4 drinks):
- Practice the Collins pattern: 4-3-2 + soda + lemon

**Then everything else** (smaller families are easier)

### Round 2: Random Order (20 min)
Put drink names on pieces of paper, shuffle, quiz yourself randomly:
- Moscow Mule → ??? → "Rocks! 4 cl vodka, juice 2 limes, Ginger Beer, lime"
- Aperol Spritz → ??? → "Wine glass! 5 Prosecco, 5 Aperol, soda, 3 oranges"

**This is the MOST IMPORTANT round** - random order = real service conditions.

---

## 🎪 PHASE 3: SCENARIO PRACTICE (20 minutes)

### Drill 1: Speed Orders (10 min)
Set a 30-second timer per drink. Can you recall the full recipe before time's up?

Go through each drink. If you miss one, mark it and come back.

### Drill 2: Substitution Practice (10 min)
Practice the phrases:

"Customer: Do you have an Espresso Martini?"
→ You: "We don't have that one, but we have an Espresso Tonic - it's espresso over tonic water with ice, very refreshing and still gives you that coffee kick! Or I can make you a White Russian which is creamy with coffee liqueur."

Practice 5 scenarios:
1. Espresso Martini → Espresso Tonic
2. Mojito → Tom Collins
3. Margarita → GT with salt rim
4. Cosmopolitan → Cape Codder
5. Old Fashioned → French Connection

**Say these OUT LOUD.** Seriously. Your brain remembers better when you speak.

---

## 🔄 PHASE 4: FINAL REVIEW (10 minutes)

### The "Walk Through" Method
Close your eyes and imagine you're behind the bar:

1. **Ice in glass** → What glass? Where's the ice?
2. **Pour spirit** → Which bottle? How much?
3. **Add modifiers** → What goes in next?
4. **Top with mixer** → Which mixer? Where is it?
5. **Garnish** → What garnish? How do I cut it?

Do this for:
- Moscow Mule (most popular)
- Tom Collins (tricky to remember)
- Aperol Spritz (unique recipe)
- White Russian (different from others)
- Cuba Libre (simple but classic)

## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Don't confuse glass types**:
   - Moscow Mule = ROCKS (not Highball!)
   - Russians = LOWBALL (not Rocks!)

2. **Don't mix up "squeeze 3 limes" vs. "juice of 2 limes"**:
   - GT + Cuba Libre = squeeze 3 limes
   - Moscow Mule = juice 2 limes (one dumped in)

3. **Don't forget the Dark & Stormy exception**:
   - It's **3 cl** rum, not 4!

4. **Collins pattern is consistent**:
   - Tom = regular syrup
   - Rhubarb = rhubarb syrup
   - Both: 4 gin, 3 lemon, 2 sweet, soda, lemon slice
