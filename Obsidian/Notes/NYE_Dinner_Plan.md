# New Year's Eve Dinner Plan
**6 Guests | Serve Time: 7:30 PM**

---

## Quick Reference

| Item | Temperature | Time |
|------|-------------|------|
| Beef sous vide (prep day) | 55°C | 3 hrs |
| Wellington Stage 1 | 230°C convection | ~40 min |
| Wellington Stage 2 | 65-93°C (lowest) | ~40 min to 40°C core |
| Wellington rest | — | 15-20 min |
| Pommes Anna warm | 160°C | 12-15 min |
| Pommes Anna sear | Med-high | 2-4 min/side |

**Critical Temps:**
- Target core during Stage 2: **40°C+** (carryover to 55°C)
- Keep meat surface below **49°C** during Stage 2

## Shopping List

### Meat/Protein
- [ ] Beef tenderloin, center-cut — **1.5 kg**
- [ ] Prosciutto or lardo, thinly sliced — **150g** (enough to wrap)

### Produce
- [ ] Cremini mushrooms — **350g**
- [ ] Shiitake mushrooms — **150g**
- [ ] Shallots — **6-8 medium** (4 for duxelles, 3-4 for sauce)
- [ ] Fresh thyme — **1 large bunch**
- [ ] Waxy potatoes (Yukon Gold or similar) — **1.2 kg**

- [ ] Lemon — **1**

- [ ] Bay leaves — **2**

- [ ] All-butter puff pastry — **500g** (1 sheet or block)
- [ ] Eggs — **2** (for wash)
- [ ] Clarified butter or ghee — **150g** (for Pommes Anna)
- [ ] Unsalted butter, cold — **60g** (for sauce finishing)

### Pantry
- [ ] Dijon mustard — **2 tbsp**
- [ ] Tomato paste — **1 tbsp**
- [ ] Beef stock, good quality — **750ml**
- [ ] Olive oil
- [ ] Flaky salt
- [ ] Black pepper

### Wine/Alcohol
- [ ] Madeira wine — **120ml** (for duxelles)

## Prep Day Timeline — Tuesday, Dec 30

### Morning: Sauce & Duxelles

- [ ] **9:00 AM** — Start red wine reduction sauce
  - Sauté 3-4 minced shallots in oil until soft
  - Add 1 tbsp tomato paste, cook 1 min
  - Add 750ml red wine, reduce by half (~20 min)
  - Add 750ml beef stock, thyme sprigs, bay leaf
  - Reduce to ~300ml (coats spoon), strain, chill

- [ ] **10:30 AM** — Make duxelles
  - Finely chop 500g mushrooms (food processor: pulse, don't purée)
  - Mince 2 shallots, strip leaves from thyme
  - Sauté shallots until soft
  - Add mushrooms, cook on med-high until **completely dry** (15-20 min)
  - Deglaze with 120ml Madeira
  - Cook until **dry again** — no moisture visible
  - Season, spread on sheet pan to cool, then chill

### Afternoon: Beef & Assembly

- [ ] **1:00 PM** — Cook beef tenderloin
  - *Option A — Sous vide:* 55°C for 3 hours
  - *Option B — Oven roast:* 120°C until internal temp reaches 55°C
  - Transfer to fridge immediately, chill **completely** (2+ hours)

- [ ] **4:00 PM** — Sear chilled beef
  - Pat completely dry
  - Sear all sides in smoking-hot pan (30 sec per side)
  - Return to fridge for 30 min to cool surface

- [ ] **5:00 PM** — Assemble Wellington
  1. Lay plastic wrap on counter
  2. Shingle prosciutto/lardo slices in rectangle
  3. Spread duxelles evenly over prosciutto
  4. Brush beef with Dijon mustard
  5. Place beef at edge, roll tightly using plastic wrap
  6. Twist ends, refrigerate 30 min to set shape

- [ ] **6:00 PM** — Wrap in puff pastry
  - Roll puff pastry to 5mm thick
  - Unwrap beef log, place on pastry
  - Roll pastry around, seal edges with egg wash
  - Tuck ends under
  - Egg wash entire surface
  - **Make OVERSIZED probe hole** (use thick skewer or chopstick)
  - Score decorative pattern if desired

- [ ] **6:30 PM** — Freeze Wellington overnight
  - Place on parchment-lined tray
  - Freeze uncovered until solid, then wrap loosely

### Evening: Pommes Anna

- [ ] **7:00 PM** — Prepare Pommes Anna
  - Preheat oven to 200°C
  - Slice potatoes 3mm thin (mandoline)
  - Brush 9" oven-safe pan generously with clarified butter
  - Layer potatoes in overlapping circles, brushing butter + salt between layers
  - Press down firmly, cover with foil
  - Bake 45 min covered, then 20-30 min uncovered until golden/tender

- [ ] **8:30 PM** — Press and chill Pommes Anna
  - Place plate on top with weight (cans)
  - Press while hot for 15 min
  - Refrigerate overnight

## Day-Of Timeline — Wednesday, Dec 31

> [!info] Work Backwards from 7:30 PM Serve Time

### 5:30 PM — Setup

- [ ] Remove Wellington from freezer (keep cold)
- [ ] Preheat oven to **230°C convection**
- [ ] Insert probe thermometer into Wellington's pre-made hole
- [ ] Set up serving plates near oven to warm later
- [ ] Take sauce out to temper slightly

### 5:50 PM — Wellington Stage 1

- [ ] Place frozen Wellington directly in **230°C** oven
- [ ] Bake until **deep golden brown** (~40 min)
- [ ] Watch pastry color — cover loosely with foil if browning too fast

### 6:30 PM — Wellington Stage 2

- [ ] Drop oven to **lowest setting** (65-93°C)
- [ ] Continue baking until core reaches **40°C+** (~40 min)
- [ ] Monitor surface temp — keep **below 49°C**

### 6:45 PM — Warm Pommes Anna

- [ ] Unmold Pommes Anna, cut into 6 wedges
- [ ] Place wedges on baking sheet
- [ ] Warm at **160°C** for 12-15 min (can share oven or use second oven)

### 7:00 PM — Prepare Sides

- [ ] Heat cast iron or heavy pan for Pommes Anna sear
- [ ] Add butter, sear wedges **2-4 min per side** until crispy
- [ ] Keep warm on plate, tented

### 7:10 PM — Wellington Out

- [ ] Remove Wellington when core hits **40°C+**
- [ ] Rest on cutting board, loosely tented — **15-20 min**
- [ ] Carryover will bring to ~55°C (perfect medium-rare)

### 7:15 PM — Sauce & Broccolini

- [ ] **Warm plates** in low oven
- [ ] Gently reheat sauce (don't boil)
- [ ] Remove from heat, **whisk in 60g cold butter** in pieces
- [ ] Season to taste, keep warm

- [ ] Heat large pan, high heat
- [ ] Add olive oil, sauté broccolini 3-4 min
- [ ] Add minced garlic last 30 seconds
- [ ] Finish with lemon juice, salt

### 7:30 PM — Plate & Serve

- [ ] Slice Wellington with sharp serrated knife (~2cm slices)
- [ ] Arrange on warm plates with Pommes Anna wedge
- [ ] Add broccolini
- [ ] Spoon sauce around (not over) Wellington
- [ ] Serve immediately

## Critical Notes

> [!warning] Pastry Integrity
> - Beef **MUST** be completely cold before wrapping in pastry
> - Duxelles **MUST** be bone-dry or pastry will be soggy
> - If in doubt, cook duxelles longer

> [!warning] Probe Hole
> Make the probe hole **OVERSIZED** before freezing — it shrinks significantly. Use a thick skewer or chopstick, not a thin probe.

> [!warning] Stage 2 Temperature
> During Stage 2, keep the meat surface **below 49°C**. The low oven allows the frozen center to catch up while carryover brings final temp to 55°C during rest.

> [!warning] Serving Temperature
> Use **hot plates** and **hot sauce** — the bake-from-frozen method can leave the center cooler than traditional methods. This compensates perfectly.

> [!tip] Make-Ahead Summary
> | Component | Prep Day | Day-Of |

> | Sauce | Fully made, chilled | Reheat + finish with butter |
> | Duxelles | Fully made, chilled | Used in assembly |
> | Wellington | Fully assembled, frozen | Bake from frozen |
> | Pommes Anna | Baked, pressed, chilled | Warm + pan sear |
> | Broccolini | — | Sauté fresh |
