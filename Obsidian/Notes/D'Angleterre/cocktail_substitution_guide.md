# Cocktail Substitution Guide
## "We don't have that, but let me offer you..."

---

## ☕ COFFEE-BASED REQUESTS

### **"Do you have an Espresso Martini?"**
**NO, BUT OFFER:**
1. **Espresso Tonic** ⭐ (BEST CHOICE)
   - "We have an Espresso Tonic - it's espresso over tonic with ice, very refreshing and still gives you that coffee kick!"
   - *Why it works*: Coffee flavor, similar energy boost, modern/trendy

2. **White Russian** (if they want creamy)
   - "I can make you a White Russian - vodka, Kahlua, and milk. It's smooth and coffee-flavored."
   - *Why it works*: Coffee liqueur, creamy, dessert-like

3. **Black Russian** (if they want it less sweet)
   - "How about a Black Russian? It's vodka and coffee liqueur, no cream - stronger coffee taste."
   - *Why it works*: Pure coffee and vodka, no sweetness

---

## 🍹 FRESH/CITRUS/MINTY REQUESTS

### **"Can I get a Mojito?"**
**NO, BUT OFFER:**
1. **Tom Collins** ⭐ (BEST CHOICE)
   - "We don't have Mojitos, but our Tom Collins is similar - gin, fresh lemon juice, and soda water with ice. Very refreshing!"
   - *Why it works*: Fresh citrus, fizzy, bright, served tall

2. **Rhubarb Collins**
   - "Or try the Rhubarb Collins - same freshness but with a unique rhubarb twist."
   - *Why it works*: Collins = refreshing + tart

3. **Vodka Soda**
   - "I can also make a simple Vodka Soda with fresh lime - clean and light."
   - *Why it works*: Simple, fresh, citrusy

---

## 🧂 TEQUILA REQUESTS

### **"Do you have Margaritas?"**
**NO, BUT OFFER:**
1. **GT (Gin Tonic) with salt rim** ⭐
   - "We don't have tequila, but I can make you a Gin Tonic with a salted rim and extra lime - gives you that citrus-salt combo!"
   - *Why it works*: Citrus + salt = margarita feel, botanical

2. **Vodka Soda**
   - "Or a Vodka Soda with lots of lime if you want something simple and citrusy."
   - *Why it works*: Clean, lime-forward

---

## 🍸 VODKA CRANBERRY VARIATIONS

### **"Can I get a Cosmopolitan?" / "Sex on the Beach?"**
**NO, BUT OFFER:**
1. **Cape Codder** ⭐ (BEST CHOICE)

## 🎯 THE SUBSTITUTION FORMULA

When a guest orders something you don't have:

### **STEP 1**: Acknowledge kindly
- "We don't have that one, but let me offer you something similar..."

### **STEP 2**: Identify what they're looking for:
- **Coffee?** → Espresso Tonic, Russians
- **Fresh/citrusy?** → Collins, Vodka Soda, GT
- **Fruity?** → Summer Breeze, Screwdriver, Madras
- **Strong/spirit-forward?** → French Connection, Whiskey Highball
- **Creamy/dessert?** → White Russian
- **Fizzy/light?** → Aperol Spritz, any highball

### **STEP 3**: Offer 1-2 options (not more!)
- Give your TOP recommendation first
- Explain WHY it's similar
- Sound confident

---

## ⚡ QUICK CHEAT PHRASES

**For any confusion:**
- "That's not on our menu tonight, but based on [flavor/style], I'd recommend..."

**For tough substitutions:**
- "We're limited on [tequila/rum/etc.] tonight, but our [X] has a similar [fresh/strong/fruity] profile."

**For mocktails:**
- "We have several great non-alcoholic options - would you like something fruity, fizzy, or refreshing?"

**When unsure:**
- "Let me describe a few of our most popular drinks and you can pick what sounds best!"

## 🎄 CHRISTMAS EVENT PRO TIPS

### What guests at Christmas events usually want:
- **Festive colors**: Aperol Spritz (orange), Cape Codder (red), White Russian (creamy white)
- **Warm flavors**: Nothing warm, but French Connection feels cozy
- **Light & bubbly**: Aperol Spritz, GT, any Collins
- **Dessert drinks**: White Russian, French Connection

### Seasonal upselling:
- "The Aperol Spritz is very festive with the orange slices!"
- "White Russian is like a liquid dessert - perfect for celebrating!"
- "Our Espresso Tonic will keep you dancing all night!"

## 🧠 FINAL MEMORIZATION TIP

**Create mental categories:**
- **Coffee crew**: Espresso Tonic, Black/White Russian
- **Fresh & fizzy**: Collins (Tom/Rhubarb), Vodka Soda, GT
- **Fruity favorites**: Summer Breeze, Screwdriver, Madras, Cape Codder
- **Spirit-forward**: French Connection, Whiskey Highball
- **Party starters**: Moscow Mule, Cuba Libre, Dark & Stormy
- **Light & bubbly**: Aperol Spritz

When someone orders off-menu, think: "Which category does their drink belong to?" Then offer from that category.
