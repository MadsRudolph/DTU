## 📏 THE 4 CL RULE
Almost everything = **4 cl base spirit**
Exceptions: Dark & Stormy (3), Russians (3), French Connection (4+4)

---

## 🥃 GLASS CODES
- **HB** = Highball (default for most)
- **R** = Rocks (Moscow Mule, Dark & Stormy)
- **LB** = Lowball (Russians, French Connection, Espresso Tonic, Virgin Bramble)
- **Wine** = Aperol Spritz
- **Champagne** = Virgin Mimosa

---

## 🍋 GARNISH QUICK RULE
- **Lime** = Almost all vodka, GT, rum drinks
- **Lemon** = Collins drinks, Whiskey Highball
- **Orange** = Aperol Spritz (3 slices!)

---

## ⚡ 3-SECOND MEMORY TRICKS

**"Squeeze 3 limes" = GT + Cuba Libre**

**Ginger Beer drinks = Moscow Mule (vodka) + Dark & Stormy (rum)**

**Collins = 4-3-2 (4 gin, 3 lemon, 2 sweet, soda, lemon slice)**

**Russians = 3-3-3 (White) or 3-4 (Black), both Lowball**

**Lowball crew = Russians, French Connection, Espresso Tonic, Virgin Bramble**
