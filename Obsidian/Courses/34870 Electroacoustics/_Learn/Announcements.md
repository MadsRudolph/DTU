## 2026-08-23 — Welcome to the course - What you need to know and prepare

Dear all,
Welcome to the Electroacoustics course.
Our first lecture will be on Monday 31st August from 13:00h, in our classroom 019, building 352 at Lyngby campus. We will initially have lectures Mondays 13.00-17.00h and Thursdays 8.30-12.00h. After a few weeks, Monday lectures will be replaced by lab exercises, guest lectures, follow-up, and other activities. Later on, Thursday lectures will also turn into practical work. Consult the 34870 Course plan Fall 2026 document for information about lectures and references. This document will be updated during the course with new activities and eventual changes.
If you do not have a background in electrical circuits, we strongly recommend you study the basics of circuit analysis: Kirchhoff's laws, descriptions of the R, L, C components and their impedances, current and voltage sources, dual circuits, Norton and Thévenin equivalents. This knowledge is available from many sources, including freely available textbooks at the library, e.g. https://findit.dtu.dk/en/catalog/2301801656.
The main references for the course are:
1 - Introduction to electroacoustics and audio amplifier design, 4th ed., by Marshall Leach. Excerpts will be distributed for your use during the course, and this, together with the slides, covers the course sufficiently, so buying this book is not necessary. If you, however, want an electronic or printed version, see https://he.kendallhunt.com/product/introduction-electroacoustics-and-audio-amplifier-design. You may also order the printed version at the DTU bookstore or use one of the few physical copies of past editions available at the library.
2 - Acoustics : Sound Fields and Transducers, by Beranek and Mellow. Covers almost all material in reference 1. PDF available from DTU Library: https://findit.dtu.dk/en/catalog/2449915568  
3 - A. Lenk et al.: Electromechanical Systems in Microtechnology and Mechatronics : Electrical, Mechanical and Acoustic Networks, their Interactions and Applications (2011), https://findit.dtu.dk/en/catalog/2488639942  
4 - BIPM brochures and documents on Metrology: Download at https://www.bipm.org/en/publications/si-brochure/
The lectures from August 31st to September 10th are about acoustical and mechanical analogies, which are physical systems represented as electrical circuits. Frieder will teach this part. You may find the theory in Leach's chapters 3 and 4, in Beranek's chapter 3, and chapters 2 and 3 in Lenk.
The lecture note Fundamentals of Acoustics is uploaded in DTU Learn (Course Content > Basic Material) for those needing a background refreshing. Review chapter 1 in particular.
During the course, we will make use of a free circuit simulation program, LTSpice: https://www.analog.com/en/design-center/design-tools-and-calculators/ltspice-simulator.html. We will only use its most basic components. You will use and learn it during the lectures, and it will be a necessary tool to solve exercises and lab tasks. We advise you have it installed on your computers for the first lecture. It works for Mac and Windows. We have also uploaded a LTspice circuit simulator - Quick Guide. If you never worked with circuits or circuit simulation before, it is highly recommended that you browse this guide and familiarize yourself with the program.
We will populate the DTU Learn - Course Content with more material such as lecture slides, exercises, lab instructions, etc. as the course progresses. Check regularly. 
See you soon!
Best regards,
Vicente, Frieder, and Finn
