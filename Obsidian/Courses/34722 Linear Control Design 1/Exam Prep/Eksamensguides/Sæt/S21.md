# Del 1 — "31. maj 2021" (Q1–Q10)

## Q1 — Blokdiagram A–F *(T2)*

**Hvad spørges om:** Find $Y/U$ for diagram med hovedvej $ABCD$, feedforward-gren $E$ (efter $A$, ind via $C,D$), feedback $F$.

> [!formula] Mason
> Forward paths: $ABCD$ og $ECD$. Eneste loop: $BCF$ (negativ).
> $$\frac{Y}{U}=\frac{ABCD+ECD}{1+BCF}$$

**Svar:** $\boxed{\dfrac{ABCD+ECD}{1+BCF} \Rightarrow \text{Mulighed 2}}$ — mulighed 1 har forkerte led; mulighed 3 mangler "1" i nævner.

→ Metode: [[Blokdiagram-reduktion]]

## Q2 — RLC → blokdiagram *(T2)*

**Hvad spørges om:** Hvilket blokdiagram svarer til serie-RLC med $V_o$ over kondensatoren?

> [!formula] KVL
> $$I=\frac{V_i-V_o}{R+sL}\ (\text{forward }\tfrac{1}{R+sL}),\qquad V_o=\frac{1}{sC}I\ (\text{forward }\tfrac{1}{sC})$$
> med negativ unity feedback fra $V_o$.

**Svar:** $\boxed{\text{Mulighed 1}}$

→ Metode: [[Blokdiagram-reduktion]] · [[Transfer-functions-og-poler]]

## Q3 — Pol/nul-kort → Bode *(T7)*

**Hvad spørges om:** To komplekse poler $-1.5\pm1.65j$ + reelt nul $-2.5$. Hvilket Bode?

**Metode:** 2 poler $-$ 1 nul = relativ grad 1 → høj-frekvens $-20$ dB/dek, fase ender $-90°$. Nul reelt og negativt → minimum-fase.

**Svar:** $\boxed{\text{Mulighed 4}}$ (negativt nul $-2.5$, negative komplekse poler).

→ Metode: [[Bode-aflæsning]]

## Q4 — P-stabilitetsrange *(T5)*

**Hvad spørges om:** $P(s)=\frac{1}{(s+1)^3}$, P-controller $K>0$. For hvilke $K$ er closed-loop stabil?

> [!formula] Bode-kriterium
> $$\angle L=-3\arctan\omega=-180° \Rightarrow \bar\omega=\tan60°=\sqrt3$$
> $$|L(j\bar\omega)|=\frac{K}{(\sqrt{1+3})^3}=\frac{K}{8}=1 \Rightarrow \bar K=8 \Rightarrow 0<K<8$$

```matlab hl:/margin\(/
s=tf('s'); L=1/(s+1)^3;
[GM,~,wcg]=margin(L);
fprintf('Kbar=GM=%.4g ved w=%.4g\n', GM, wcg);   % 8 ved sqrt(3)
```

**Svar:** $\boxed{0<K<8 \Rightarrow \text{Mulighed 3}}$

→ Metode: [[Stabilitet-Nyquist-margins]]

## Q5 — Bode → TF (RHP-pol) *(T7)*

**Hvad spørges om:** DC=40 dB, $-40$ dB/dek ved $\omega=1$, så $-60$ dB/dek ved $\omega=10$; fase ender $-90°$. Hvilket udsagn?

**Metode:** DC=100. To poler ved $\omega=1$. Ved $\omega=10$ falder hældningen yderligere men fasen *stiger* → **RHP-pol** ved $+10$.

> [!formula] System
> $$G(s)=\frac{100}{(1+s)^2(1-0.1s)} \quad(\text{poler }-1,-1,+10)$$

**Svar:** $\boxed{\text{Negative OG positive reelle poler, ingen nuller} \Rightarrow \text{Mulighed 1}}$

→ Metode: [[Bode-aflæsning]]

## Q6 — $K$ for PM=40° *(T8)*

**Hvad spørges om:** $L(s)=\frac{K}{s(s+3)(s+10)}$. Ved $\omega=2.28$ er fase $-140°$ og $|L/K|=-38.9$ dB. Find $K$ for PM=40°.

> [!formula] 0-dB-betingelse
> $$\text{Ved fase}=-140°\ (\text{PM}=40°):\quad K=10^{38.9/20}=88$$

```matlab hl:N
fprintf('K = %.4g\n', 10^(38.9/20));   % 88.1
```

**Svar:** $\boxed{K=88 \Rightarrow \text{Mulighed 5}}$ — $19.5=38.9/2$; $38.9$ er dB-tallet brugt som $K$.

→ Metode: [[Controller-design-P-PI-Lead]] · [[Bode-aflæsning]]

## Q7 — To masser + fjeder *(T11)*

**Hvad spørges om:** $m_1=m_2=1$, $k=1$, kraft $F$ på $m_1$. Find $X_2/F$.

> [!formula] Bevægelsesligninger
> $$m_1\ddot x_1+k(x_1-x_2)=F,\quad m_2\ddot x_2+k(x_2-x_1)=0$$
> $$\frac{X_2}{F}=\frac{k}{(m_2s^2+k)(m_1s^2+k)-k^2}=\frac{1}{(s^2+1)^2-1}=\frac{1}{s^2(s^2+2)}$$

**Svar:** $\boxed{\dfrac{X_2}{F}=\dfrac{1}{s^2(s^2+2)} \Rightarrow \text{Mulighed 2}}$

→ Metode: [[Transfer-functions-og-poler]]

## Q8 — ODE → poler *(T1)*

**Hvad spørges om:** $\ddot y+2\dot y+y=u$. Find polerne.

> [!formula] Karakteristisk
> $$s^2+2s+1=(s+1)^2=0 \Rightarrow s=-1\ (\text{dobbelt})$$

```matlab hl:/roots\(/
disp(roots([1 2 1]).')   % -1, -1
```

**Svar:** $\boxed{s_1=s_2=-1 \Rightarrow \text{Mulighed 2}}$

→ Metode: [[Transfer-functions-og-poler]]

## Q9 — Overshoot ≤12% → $K$ *(T3)*

> [!warning] OCR
> Facit trykker $M_p=e^{-\zeta\omega_n/\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}}$, men korrekt er $\boxed{e^{-\pi\zeta/\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}}}$ (beregningen bruger $\pi$).

**Hvad spørges om:** Unity feedback $\frac{K}{s(s+5)}$. Find $K$ så overshoot $\le12\%$.

> [!formula] $\zeta$-krav → $K$
> $$\frac{Y}{R}=\frac{K}{s^2+5s+K},\ \omega_n=\sqrt K,\ \zeta=\frac{2.5}{\sqrt K}$$
> $$M_p\le0.12 \Rightarrow \zeta\ge0.559 \Rightarrow K=\frac{6.25}{\zeta^2}\le20$$

```matlab hl:N
zmin = -log(0.12)/sqrt(pi^2+log(0.12)^2);
fprintf('zeta_min=%.4g, Kmax=%.4g\n', zmin, 6.25/zmin^2);
```

**Svar:** $\boxed{0\le K\le20 \Rightarrow \text{Mulighed 1}}$

→ Metode: [[Time-response-2nd-order]] · [[Controller-design-P-PI-Lead]]

## Q10 — Magnitude-Bode → step *(T3/T7)*

**Hvad spørges om:** Magnitude $20$ dB → $0$ dB (zero + pol), ingen RHP-nuller. Hvilken step-respons?

> [!formula] System + step
> $$G(s)=\frac{s+100}{s+10},\quad y(t)=10-9e^{-10t}\ \Rightarrow\ y(0^+)=1,\ y(\infty)=10$$

```matlab hl:/step\(/
step(tf([1 100],[1 10]))   % 1 -> 10
```

**Svar:** $\boxed{\text{Starter ved 1, stiger til 10} \Rightarrow \text{Mulighed 1}}$

→ Metode: [[Bode-aflæsning]] · [[Time-response-2nd-order]]

# Del 2 — Exam Questionnaire (Q11–Q20)

## Q11 — Bode → $K_P$-range *(T8)*

$G(s)=\frac{120}{s^3+43s^2+120s}$. Ved $\omega_{180}=10.96$ er $|G|=-32.68$ dB. Gain margin $=10^{32.68/20}\approx43$ → stabil for $0<K_P<43$.

**Svar:** $\boxed{K_P=25 \Rightarrow \text{b}}$ (eneste positive $<43$).

```matlab hl:/margin\(/
s=tf('s'); G=120/(s^3+43*s^2+120*s); [GM]=margin(G);
fprintf('Kp_max = %.4g\n', GM);
```

→ Metode: [[Stabilitet-Nyquist-margins]]

## Q12 — ODE → stabilitet *(T1)*

$y^{(4)}+9y^{(3)}+20\ddot y=71u \Rightarrow G=\frac{71}{s^2(s^2+9s+20)}$. Dobbeltpol i origo → **ustabil**, DC-gain $=\infty$.

**Svar:** $\boxed{\text{Ustabil} \Rightarrow \text{d}}$

→ Metode: [[Transfer-functions-og-poler]] · [[Stabilitet-Nyquist-margins]]

## Q13 — Nyquist → $K_P$ (RHP-pol) *(T8)*

$G(s)=\frac{s+1}{s^2+43s-90}$ har 1 RHP-pol ($s=+2.05$). Nyquist krydser CCW ved $-0.0111$ → $K_P>1/0.0111\approx90$.

**Svar:** $\boxed{K_P=100 \Rightarrow \text{a}}$

→ Metode: [[Stabilitet-Nyquist-margins]]

## Q14 — Phase margin fra Nyquist *(T8)*

$G(s)=\frac{1200}{s^3+54s^2+570s+400}$. Krydser enhedscirkel ved $(0.134,-0.99)$ → $\angle G=\arctan(-0.99/0.134)\approx-82.3°$, så $\gamma_M=180°-82.3°=97.7°$.

**Svar:** $\boxed{\gamma_M=97.7° \Rightarrow \text{e}}$

```matlab hl:N
fprintf('PM = %.4g grader\n', 180 + atan2d(-0.99,0.134));
```

→ Metode: [[Stabilitet-Nyquist-margins]]

## Q15 — P-control koncept *(type-0)*

2.-ordens type-0, 2 reelle poler, P-controller. $K_P$ påvirker **ikke** fasen, men løfter magnitude → crossover stiger.

**Svar:** $\boxed{\text{Øget }K_P\text{ øger crossover-frekvensen} \Rightarrow \text{b}}$

→ Metode: [[Bode-aflæsning]] · [[Controller-design-P-PI-Lead]]

## Q16 — Steady-state (P i feedback) *(T4)*

$G(s)=\frac{1224}{s^3+30s^2+257s+612}$, $K_P=2$ i feedback. $G(0)=1224/612=2$.

> [!formula] FVT
> $$e_{ss}=\frac{1}{1+K_P G(0)}=\frac{1}{1+2\cdot2}=0.2$$

**Svar:** $\boxed{e_{ss}=0.2 \Rightarrow \text{c}}$

→ Metode: [[Steady-state-error-og-disturbance]]

## Q17 — Lead-Lag → $\beta$ *(T10)*

$G(s)=\frac{5(s+7)(s+12)}{(s+4)(s+9)(s+17)(s+5)(s+70)}$, P-Lead-Lag, $\alpha=0.2$, $\omega_c=15$, $N_i=3$, $\gamma_M=70°$. Ved $\omega_c$: $\phi_G=-142.89°$.

> [!formula] Fasebudget → $\beta$
> $$\phi_L=-180°+\gamma_M-\phi_G-\arcsin\tfrac{1-\alpha}{1+\alpha}\approx-8.9°,\quad \arctan\!\Big(N_i\tfrac{1-\beta}{1+\beta N_i^2}\Big)=\phi_L \Rightarrow \beta\approx2$$

**Svar:** $\boxed{\beta\approx2 \Rightarrow \text{b}}$

→ Metode: [[Controller-design-P-PI-Lead]]

## Q18 — PI-Lead → $\alpha,K_P$ *(T10)*

$G(s)=\frac{0.7(s+0.5)}{(5s+1)(s^2+0.2s+0.6)(0.01s+1)}$, $\gamma_M=45°$, $\omega_c=10$, $N_i=8$. Ved $\omega_c$: $\phi_G=-184.98°$.

> [!formula] Fasebudget → $\alpha$
> $$\phi_d=-180°+\gamma_M-\phi_G+\arctan\tfrac1{N_i}=58.4° \Rightarrow \alpha=\frac{1-\sin\phi_d}{1+\sin\phi_d}=0.08$$
> $$\tau_i=0.8,\ \tau_d=0.354,\ K_P=\frac{1}{|G C_I C_D(j\omega_c)|}=200$$

```matlab hl:/evalfr\(/
s=tf('s'); G=0.7*(s+0.5)/((5*s+1)*(s^2+0.2*s+0.6)*(0.01*s+1));
gM=45; Ni=8; wc=10;
phiG=rad2deg(angle(evalfr(G,1j*wc)));
phid=-180+gM-phiG+atand(1/Ni);
alpha=(1-sind(phid))/(1+sind(phid));
taui=Ni/wc; taud=1/(wc*sqrt(alpha));
CPI=(taui*s+1)/(taui*s); CD=(taud*s+1)/(alpha*taud*s+1);
Kp=1/abs(evalfr(G*CPI*CD,1j*wc));
fprintf('alpha=%.4g, Kp=%.4g\n', alpha, Kp);
```

**Svar:** $\boxed{\alpha=0.08,\ K_P=200 \Rightarrow \text{d}}$

→ Metode: [[Controller-design-P-PI-Lead]]

## Q19 — Indre/ydre loop → $K_2$ *(T4)*

$G_2(0)=10^{-7.96/20}=0.4$. Åben ydre loop: $\varepsilon_1=0.4$. Mål: $\varepsilon_2=0.05$.

> [!formula] Løs for $K_2$
> $$K_2=\frac{1-\varepsilon_2}{\varepsilon_2\,G_2(0)\,(1-\varepsilon_1)}=\frac{0.95}{0.05\cdot0.4\cdot0.6}=79.17$$

**Svar:** $\boxed{K_2=79.17 \Rightarrow \text{e}}$

→ Metode: [[Steady-state-error-og-disturbance]]

## Q20 — Disturbance feed-forward *(T4)*

$K_P=1$, $G(s)=\frac{21(0.5s+1)}{s^2+4s+21}$, $D(s)=\frac{1}{0.01s+1}$. Ufiltreret feed-forward $F_d=D/G$ (stabil + proper) → $F_d G=D$ → perfekt annullering.

> [!formula] Sensitivitet
> $$G_{yd}=\frac{F_d G-D}{1+K_P G}=\frac{D-D}{1+K_P G}=0$$

**Svar:** $\boxed{G_{yd}=0 \Rightarrow \text{a}}$

→ Metode: [[Steady-state-error-og-disturbance]] · Teori: [[Lec 13 — Feed-forward Control]]
