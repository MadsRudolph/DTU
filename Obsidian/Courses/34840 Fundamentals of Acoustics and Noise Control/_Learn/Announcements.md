## 2026-07-08 — Welcome to Fundamentals of Acoustics and Noise Control.

Dear all 
Welcome to this course on Fundamentals of Acoustics and Noise Control.
The course material consists of Fundamentals_of_acoustics_2022 and Introduction to Loudspeakers -2021
In this course we expect students to be familiar with basic complex numbers math and to have an understanding of the basic concepts in signal analysis and processing. Some of you come from a BSc program where you have exercised these concepts and methods extensively, but others not so much.
Therefore I've prepared and uploaded some material that covers the background knowledge that you will need in this course. All of the material is in the '00 - Prerequisites'  folder. The material includes
Complex numbers :  theory recap document, problems and solutions 
Signal and Systems:  Slides and video lectures that cover the basics of signal analysis and systems understanding
I strongly recommend that you look at this materials as preparation for the course, this also applies if you have learnt these things previously, but havent used them actively for quite some time.
The lecture plan will be available in August
See you in September
Finn Agerkvist
