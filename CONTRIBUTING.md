# Contributing to this repo

First of all: this is a **personal DTU study repo**, not a polished public project.  
If you're here and still want to help improve things — thanks 🙌

---

## Scope

This repository mainly contains:

- Course notes and summaries  
- Derivations, cheat sheets, and reference material  
- Small helper scripts and simulations  

Contributions are welcome **only** in the spirit of:

- Fixing mistakes (math, typos, broken references)
- Clarifying explanations
- Improving structure or readability of notes

This is **not** a solutions bank and should not become one.

---

## Academic integrity

By contributing, you agree to respect:

- The repo’s [Academic integrity & usage](./README.md#academic-integrity--usage)
- Your own institution’s rules on academic honesty and plagiarism

Please **do not**:

- Add full solutions to active assignments/exams
- Upload material that others might misuse as a direct hand-in

If in doubt, keep it as *example-style* or leave it out.

---

## How to propose changes

You can use any of these:

- **Issues** — for:
  - reporting mistakes (wrong sign, wrong factor, wrong result)
  - suggesting small improvements (better explanation, more clear notation)
- **Pull requests** — for:
  - small corrections (typos, minor math fixes, broken links)
  - light refactoring of notes (headings, structure, TOC alignment)

Please keep changes focused and small where possible.

---

## Style guidelines (soft rules)

Not strict, but nice if you follow them:

- Markdown first: human-readable, Obsidian-friendly  
- Use standard math notation (LaTeX-style where relevant)  
- Prefer explanations + derivations over just dumping final answers  
- Don’t rename large folder structures without a very good reason
